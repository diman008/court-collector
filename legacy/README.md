`diman008/court-collector` for traceability. They include a prototype
KAD-proxy and rules/regex experiments.

They are **not** supported APIs, validated court connectors, or safe deployment
templates. The ScraperAPI proxy fallback and other old web-access logic are
**not used** by `judicial_intelligence/`. Do not run these prototypes without
a fresh security, compliance and maintenance review.
